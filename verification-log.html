Common questions about this log

What does a PASS actually confirm?

That on the stated date and time, entering the code into a real guest cart at growthguys.com reduced the subtotal by exactly 10% before payment. Nothing more, nothing less.

What does a PASS not prove?

It isn't a guarantee for every future order, every product, or every cart size — only that this specific observation happened at this specific timestamp. Storefront conditions can change after a test runs.

What happens if a scheduled check fails?

The failure is published here too, not hidden. A FAIL does not get a new stamped date on the homepage or codes list — those only update after all four codes pass the same run.

Why does the exact date and time matter?

A cadence word like "verified daily" is a claim. A specific dated, timestamped result with the retained cart record is evidence — the record can be opened and checked, not just taken on trust.

How often does this actually run?

Every day, automatically, via a scheduled job — not on request and not only when someone happens to check. See the full timestamped history below; gaps or failures are visible, not edited out.

Is this the same as an order confirmation?

No. Every test stops at the guest-cart stage — no order is placed, no payment method is entered. It measures whether the discount applies, not the full checkout or delivery experience.
